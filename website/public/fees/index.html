Fees

Kyro is free. Swaps from 0.7%.

Sending, receiving and staking cost only the network fee. Swaps carry a Kyro fee based on the swap value, already out of the amount you receive.

Swap fee by swap value

The Kyro fee is a percentage of the swap. Small swaps pay a higher rate and large swaps a lower one. The value is the size of the swap in US dollars.

How it is calculated The table gives the rate at each listed value. Between two values the rate moves smoothly, interpolated on a logarithmic scale, so there are no sudden jumps.

Unknown token price If Kyro cannot find a price for the token, it cannot work out the swap value. The fee is then 0.9%.

Already included The fee is deducted from the amount you receive. Kyro shows only what you send and what you receive, so the “you receive” amount is what arrives, with no further Kyro charge.

How the swap fee works

  • The rate depends on the swap value, from 0.7% to 5%. It is already part of the quote: the amount you receive is after the fee.
  • It is collected by each provider’s official integrator fee option. Exolix has none: on Exolix routes (Monero, Kaspa, Algorand and NEAR) the fee is a second transfer on the source chain.
  • Quotes are compared after fees: the best price shown already includes them.
  • If a provider cannot apply the fee correctly, its quote is not shown.
  • Providers may charge their own fee. It is already in the price they quote.

Network fees

Every blockchain charges a fee to process a transaction. It goes to the network’s miners or validators, not to Kyro. Kyro estimates it and shows it before you confirm. On Ethereum layer 2s like Arbitrum, Optimism and Base it is usually a few cents; on Bitcoin and Ethereum it changes with demand.

Why a swap fee

The fee pays for development. Kyro is open source, so the fee is public: the schedule is set in the code, and anyone can build a version without it. Full details are in SWAP_FEES.md.

Exolix routes

One route, two transfers

Monero, Kaspa, Algorand and NEAR go through Exolix, which has no integrator fee.

Same rate

The curve is the same as on every other route, taken from the amount you sell.

Second transfer

The Kyro fee is a second ordinary transfer of the sold coin to Kyro’s fee address on that chain. It goes right after the deposit and carries its own network fee.

No separate transfer elsewhere

On other routes the fee is collected through the provider’s official integrator mechanism.
