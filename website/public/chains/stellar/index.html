Stellar desktop wallet · XLM

Stellar wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap XLM and USDC tokens, and sign with a Ledger or Trezor. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

What you can do on Stellar

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive XLM and USDC tokens, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap into XLM from other chains (Stellar is a destination only for now). The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%, and the provider’s own costs.

    Yes

  • Earn

    Kyro offers no staking or lending on Stellar.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign Stellar transactions with a Ledger or Trezor.

    Yes

Details

Stellar in Kyro

  • Stellar accounts need a minimum balance of XLM to exist on the network.
  • USDC on Stellar is supported. The account needs a USDC trustline to hold it.
  • Stellar has no native staking.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 35 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.

Questions

Stellar questions

Is Kyro a free Stellar wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send XLM, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap XLM in Kyro?

You can swap into it, not out of it yet. Providers: 1Click, arrival only: a Stellar deposit needs a memo. Routes and limits.

Can I earn on Stellar in Kyro?

Kyro offers no staking or lending on Stellar.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on Stellar?

Yes, with a Ledger or Trezor. Device table.

What can a server see when I use Stellar in Kyro?

The server sees the addresses and transactions you ask about, and the IP of a Tor exit relay, not yours. Stellar has its own circuit family, except that all EVM networks share one. Who sees what.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for Stellar?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
