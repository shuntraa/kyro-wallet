Monero desktop wallet · XMR

Monero wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap XMR. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

What you can do on Monero

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive XMR, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap XMR both ways through Exolix, without an account. Kyro's fee is a second ordinary transfer of the coin you sell. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%, and the provider’s own costs.

    Yes

  • Earn

    Kyro offers no staking or lending on Monero.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Not supported on Monero yet. Use a software account.

    No

Details

Monero in Kyro

  • Your Kyro recovery phrase restores your Monero wallet too. To move it to another Monero wallet, Kyro can also show the matching 25-word Monero phrase, which works in Monero GUI, Feather and Cake.
  • Balance and history are scanned on your computer, so your view key never leaves it. Kyro scans against a regular monerod node, over Tor.
  • The first sync of an old restored wallet can take a while.
  • Monero has no native staking.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 35 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.

Questions

Monero questions

Is Kyro a free Monero wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send XMR, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap XMR in Kyro?

Yes, in both directions. Providers: Exolix. Kyro’s fee is a second transfer. Routes and limits.

Can I earn on Monero in Kyro?

Kyro offers no staking or lending on Monero.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on Monero?

No. Monero is not supported on hardware wallets in Kyro. Use a software account.

What can a server see when I use Monero in Kyro?

The node sees the blocks you download and the transactions you publish, from a Tor exit relay. It never gets your addresses or your view key, because Kyro scans blocks locally. Details.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for Monero?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
