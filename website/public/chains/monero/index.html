Monero · XMR

Monero wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap XMR. Kyro is free, open source and keeps your keys on your own computer.

Download Kyro All blockchains

What you can do on Monero

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive XMR, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap XMR in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Kyro does not offer staking for XMR.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Not supported on Monero yet. Use a software account.

    No

Details

Monero in Kyro

  • Your Kyro recovery phrase restores your Monero wallet too. To move it to another Monero wallet, Kyro can also show the matching 25-word Monero phrase, which works in Monero GUI, Feather and Cake.
  • Balance and history are scanned on your computer, so your view key never leaves it. Kyro scans against a regular monerod node, over Tor.
  • The first sync of an old restored wallet can take a while.
  • Monero has no native staking.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 35 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.
