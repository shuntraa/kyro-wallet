Questions

TRON questions

Is Kyro a free TRON wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send TRX, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap TRX in Kyro?

Yes, in both directions. Providers: 1Click. Exolix. Routes and limits.

Can I stake TRX in Kyro?

Yes, from a recovery-phrase account only. Stake TRX (Stake 2.0) for energy or bandwidth, then vote for Super Representatives with your TRON Power. Unstaking takes 14 days. Votes do not put your TRX at risk. Rates are estimates, never guaranteed.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on TRON?

Yes, with a Ledger. Device table.

What can a server see when I use TRON in Kyro?

The server sees the addresses and transactions you ask about, and the IP of a Tor exit relay, not yours. TRON has its own circuit family, except that all EVM networks share one. Who sees what.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for TRON?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
