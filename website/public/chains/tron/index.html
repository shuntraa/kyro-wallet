TRON · TRX

TRON wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive, swap and stake TRX and TRC-20 tokens, and sign with a Ledger. Kyro is free, open source and keeps your keys on your own computer.

Download Kyro All blockchains

What you can do on TRON

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive TRX and TRC-20 tokens, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap TRX in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Stake TRX from your own account. Kyro takes no fee on staking.

    Yes

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign TRON transactions with a Ledger.

    Yes

Details

TRON in Kyro

Stake TRX (Stake 2.0) for energy or bandwidth, then vote for Super Representatives with your TRON Power. Unstaking takes 14 days. Votes do not put your TRX at risk.

  • TRX and TRC-20 tokens such as USDT.
  • Energy and bandwidth from staking lower the cost of TRC-20 transfers.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 36 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.
