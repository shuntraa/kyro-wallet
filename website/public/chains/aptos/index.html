Questions

Aptos questions

Is Kyro a free Aptos wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send APT, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap APT in Kyro?

Yes, in both directions. Providers: 1Click. Routes and limits.

Can I stake APT in Kyro?

Yes, from a recovery-phrase account. Delegate APT to a pool, at least 11 APT. To get it back, unlock and wait for the end of the pool's lockup (up to 14 days). The operator cannot take your APT. Rates are estimates, never guaranteed.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on Aptos?

Yes, with a Ledger. Device table.

What can a server see when I use Aptos in Kyro?

The server sees the addresses and transactions you ask about, and the IP of a Tor exit relay, not yours. Aptos has its own circuit family, except that all EVM networks share one. Who sees what.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for Aptos?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
