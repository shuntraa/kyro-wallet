Questions

Polkadot questions

Is Kyro a free Polkadot wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send DOT, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap DOT in Kyro?

Not yet. 1Click does not list Polkadot, and delivery to Asset Hub is not confirmed through Exolix.

Can I earn on Polkadot in Kyro?

Kyro offers no staking or lending on Polkadot.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on Polkadot?

No. Polkadot is not supported on hardware wallets in Kyro. Use a software account.

What can a server see when I use Polkadot in Kyro?

The server sees the addresses and transactions you ask about, and the IP of a Tor exit relay, not yours. Polkadot has its own circuit family, except that all EVM networks share one. Who sees what.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for Polkadot?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
