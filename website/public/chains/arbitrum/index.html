Arbitrum desktop wallet · ETH

Arbitrum wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap ETH and ERC-20 tokens on Arbitrum, and sign with a Ledger or Trezor. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

What you can do on Arbitrum

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive ETH and ERC-20 tokens on Arbitrum, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap ETH in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Kyro does not offer staking for ETH.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign Arbitrum transactions with a Ledger or Trezor.

    Yes

Arbitrum in Kyro

  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.
