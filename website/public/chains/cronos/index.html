Cronos desktop wallet · CRO

Cronos wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap CRO and CRC-20 tokens, and sign with a Ledger or Trezor. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

What you can do on Cronos

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive CRO and CRC-20 tokens, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap CRO in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Kyro does not offer staking for CRO.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign Cronos transactions with a Ledger or Trezor.

    Yes

Cronos in Kyro
