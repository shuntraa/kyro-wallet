Avalanche desktop wallet · AVAX

Avalanche wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap AVAX and ERC-20 tokens, and sign with a Ledger or Trezor. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

Download Kyro All blockchains

What you can do on Avalanche

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive AVAX and ERC-20 tokens, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap AVAX in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Kyro does not offer staking for AVAX.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign Avalanche transactions with a Ledger or Trezor.

    Yes

Details

Avalanche in Kyro

  • Kyro uses the Avalanche C-Chain.
  • Same address as your Ethereum account. Network fees are paid in AVAX.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 35 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.
