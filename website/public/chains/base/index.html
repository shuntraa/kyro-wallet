Base desktop wallet · ETH

Base wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap ETH and ERC-20 tokens on Base, and sign with a Ledger or Trezor. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

Questions

Base questions

Is Kyro a free Base wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send ETH, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap ETH in Kyro?

Yes, in both directions. Providers: 0x and LI.FI on the same chain. 1Click and LI.FI across chains. Exolix to XMR, KAS, ALGO and NEAR. Routes and limits.

Can I earn on Base in Kyro?

Yes: Lending USDC on Aave. Rates are estimates, never guaranteed, and contract risks apply. Options and risks.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on Base?

Yes, with a Ledger or Trezor. Device table.

What can a server see when I use Base in Kyro?

The server sees the addresses and transactions you ask about, and the IP of a Tor exit relay, not yours. Base has its own circuit family, except that all EVM networks share one. Who sees what.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for Base?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
