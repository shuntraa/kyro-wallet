TON desktop wallet · GRAM

TON wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap GRAM and USDT (jetton) tokens, and sign with a Ledger. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

Download Kyro All blockchains

What you can do on TON

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive GRAM and USDT (jetton) tokens, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap GRAM in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Kyro does not offer staking for GRAM.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign TON transactions with a Ledger. The Ledger wallet has a different address than the software wallet. Comments: printable ASCII, up to 120 characters.

    Yes

Details

TON in Kyro

  • TON is the network. Its coin is Gram (GRAM), renamed from Toncoin in June 2026.
  • USDT on TON is supported.
  • v4R2 wallet. The same recovery phrase gives the same address as Trust Wallet. Tonkeeper uses its own phrase format, so its addresses will not match.
  • The wallet contract is deployed on your first send.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 35 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.
