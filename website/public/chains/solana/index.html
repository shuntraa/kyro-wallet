Questions

Solana questions

Is Kyro a free Solana wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send SOL, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap SOL in Kyro?

Yes, in both directions. Providers: Jupiter on Solana. LI.FI and 1Click across chains. Exolix for SOL, USDT and USDC. Routes and limits.

Can I stake SOL in Kyro?

Yes, from a recovery-phrase account. It is refused from a hardware wallet. Delegate SOL to a validator from a stake account in your name. Activation and cooldown take about one epoch each (around two days). A validator cannot take your SOL; your rewards depend on its commission and uptime. Rates are estimates, never guaranteed.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on Solana?

Yes, with a Ledger or Trezor. Device table.

What can a server see when I use Solana in Kyro?

The server sees the addresses and transactions you ask about, and the IP of a Tor exit relay, not yours. Solana has its own circuit family, except that all EVM networks share one. Who sees what.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for Solana?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
