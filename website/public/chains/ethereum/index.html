Ethereum desktop wallet · ETH

Ethereum wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap ETH and ERC-20 tokens, and sign with a Ledger or Trezor. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

What you can do on Ethereum

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive ETH and ERC-20 tokens, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap ETH in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Kyro does not offer staking for ETH.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign Ethereum transactions with a Ledger or Trezor.

    Yes

Details

Ethereum in Kyro

  • ETH and ERC-20 tokens in the same account.
  • Token approvals are set to the exact amount by default. Unlimited approvals are off unless you turn them on.
  • Contract calls, approvals and high fees ask for a second confirmation.
  • Native ETH staking is not offered: it needs 32 ETH and a validator running at all times, and Kyro does not route your coins through a third-party contract.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 35 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.
