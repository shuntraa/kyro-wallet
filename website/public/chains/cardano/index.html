Questions

Cardano questions

Is Kyro a free Cardano wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send ADA, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap ADA in Kyro?

Yes, in both directions. Providers: 1Click. Routes and limits.

Can I stake ADA in Kyro?

Yes, from a recovery-phrase account. It is refused from a hardware wallet. Delegate to a stake pool. Your ADA stays on your address and remains spendable. Registering costs a 2 ADA deposit, returned when you stop. Rates are estimates, never guaranteed.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on Cardano?

Yes, with a Ledger or Trezor. Not on the Trezor One. A Ledger gives different Cardano addresses than the software wallet for the same phrase. Device table.

What can a server see when I use Cardano in Kyro?

The server sees the addresses and transactions you ask about, and the IP of a Tor exit relay, not yours. Cardano has its own circuit family, except that all EVM networks share one. Who sees what.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for Cardano?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
