Mantle desktop wallet · MNT

Mantle wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap MNT and ERC-20 tokens, and sign with a Ledger or Trezor. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

Questions

Mantle questions

Is Kyro a free Mantle wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send MNT, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap MNT in Kyro?

Yes, in both directions. Providers: 0x or LI.FI, same chain only. Routes and limits.

Can I earn on Mantle in Kyro?

Kyro offers no staking or lending on Mantle.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on Mantle?

Yes, with a Ledger or Trezor. Device table.

What can a server see when I use Mantle in Kyro?

The server sees the addresses and transactions you ask about, and the IP of a Tor exit relay, not yours. Mantle has its own circuit family, except that all EVM networks share one. Who sees what.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for Mantle?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
