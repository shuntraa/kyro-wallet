XRP Ledger desktop wallet · XRP

XRP Ledger wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap XRP and issued currencies, and sign with a Ledger. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

Download Kyro All blockchains

What you can do on XRP Ledger

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive XRP and issued currencies, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap XRP in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Kyro does not offer staking for XRP.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign XRP Ledger transactions with a Ledger.

    Yes

Details

XRP Ledger in Kyro

  • XRP and issued currencies.
  • Destination tags for exchanges and custodial services.
  • Every XRP Ledger account keeps a small reserve that cannot be sent.
  • The XRP Ledger has no native staking.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 35 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.
