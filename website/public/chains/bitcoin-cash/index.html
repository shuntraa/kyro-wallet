Bitcoin Cash · BCH

Bitcoin Cash wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap BCH, and sign with a Ledger or Trezor. Kyro is free, open source and keeps your keys on your own computer.

Download Kyro All blockchains

What you can do on Bitcoin Cash

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive BCH, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap BCH in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Kyro does not offer staking for BCH.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign Bitcoin Cash transactions with a Ledger or Trezor. Ledger: main network only.

    Yes

Details

Bitcoin Cash in Kyro

  • Bitcoin Cash has no native staking.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 35 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.
