Conflux in Kyro

  • Kyro uses Conflux eSpace, the EVM-compatible side of Conflux.
  • Same address as your Ethereum account. Network fees are paid in CFX.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 35 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.
