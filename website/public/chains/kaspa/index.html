Questions

Kaspa questions

Is Kyro a free Kaspa wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send KAS, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap KAS in Kyro?

Yes, in both directions. Providers: Exolix. Kyro’s fee is a second transfer. Routes and limits.

Can I earn on Kaspa in Kyro?

Kyro offers no staking or lending on Kaspa.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on Kaspa?

No. Kaspa is not supported on hardware wallets in Kyro. Use a software account.

What can a server see when I use Kaspa in Kyro?

The server sees the addresses and transactions you ask about, and the IP of a Tor exit relay, not yours. Kaspa has its own circuit family, except that all EVM networks share one. Who sees what.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for Kaspa?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
