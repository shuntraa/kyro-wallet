Sonic desktop wallet · S

Sonic wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap S and ERC-20 tokens, and sign with a Ledger or Trezor. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

What you can do on Sonic

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive S and ERC-20 tokens, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap S in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Kyro does not offer staking for S.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign Sonic transactions with a Ledger or Trezor.

    Yes
