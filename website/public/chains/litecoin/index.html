What you can do on Litecoin

  • Send and receive

    Send and receive LTC, with address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks.

    Yes

  • Swap

    Swap LTC in the app. The amount you receive already includes Kyro’s swap fee, from 0.7%.

    Yes

  • Staking

    Kyro does not offer staking for LTC.

    No

  • Hardware wallet

    Sign Litecoin transactions with a Ledger or Trezor. Ledger: main network only.

    Yes

Details

Litecoin in Kyro

  • Native SegWit (ltc1…) addresses.
  • Litecoin has no native staking.
  • Same recovery phrase as your other 35 blockchains.
  • Keys encrypted on your computer; every signature confirmed in a system window.
