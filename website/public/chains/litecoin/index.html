Litecoin desktop wallet · LTC

Litecoin wallet for Windows, macOS and Linux

Send, receive and swap LTC, and sign with a Ledger or Trezor. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial: your keys stay on your own computer and traffic goes over Tor by default.

Questions

Litecoin questions

Is Kyro a free Litecoin wallet?

Yes. Kyro is free, open source and non-custodial. You pay the network fee when you send LTC, and your keys stay on your own computer.

Can I swap LTC in Kyro?

Yes, in both directions. Providers: 1Click. Exolix. Routes and limits.

Can I earn on Litecoin in Kyro?

Kyro offers no staking or lending on Litecoin.

Does Kyro work with a hardware wallet on Litecoin?

Yes, with a Ledger or Trezor. Ledger: main network only. Device table.

What can a server see when I use Litecoin in Kyro?

The server sees the addresses and transactions you ask about, and the IP of a Tor exit relay, not yours. Litecoin has its own circuit family, except that all EVM networks share one. Who sees what.

Can I use my existing recovery phrase for Litecoin?

Yes, if it is a standard 12 or 24-word BIP39 phrase. It covers all 36 blockchains. More questions.
