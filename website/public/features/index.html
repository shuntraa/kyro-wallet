Features

What Kyro does

A private crypto wallet: send, receive, swap and stake on 36 blockchains from one recovery phrase, over Tor, and read every transaction before it is signed.

Portfolio

All your coins in one list

Total value, a price chart from one day to all time, and every asset with its balance, 7-day trend and share.

  • Market tab with the top 200 coins, and favourites.
  • Search assets and addresses with Ctrl K.
  • Allocation by asset, and a vault panel with your security status.
  • Several wallets on one device, each with its own phrase, password and history.
  • Watch addresses: follow any public address or xpub with no key.
  • Alerts for price levels and funds received, computed on your device.

Swap

The fee in plain sight

Kyro asks several providers for a quote, including NEAR Intents across chains and Exolix for Monero, then picks the best price after fees. The Kyro fee, from 0.7%, and the provider’s own costs are already out of the amount you receive. How it is calculated.

  • Token approvals are for the exact amount. Unlimited approvals are off unless you turn them on.
  • The transaction is simulated just before you sign it.
  • Maximum slippage of 0.1%, 0.5%, 1% or your own value.
  • The provider’s transaction is decoded and checked: recipient, minimum amount and fee.

Earn

16 ways to earn

Stake, liquid-stake or lend from your own accounts, in one list. Rates are estimates, never guaranteed.

  • Staking: SOL, ADA, TRX, NEAR, SUI and APT. A validator, pool or Super Representative cannot spend your coins.
  • Liquid staking: ETH with Lido, with unstaking through Lido’s queue.
  • Lending: Aave v3 on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon and Base.

Contract bugs, depegs and lower rewards are real risks. Staking works from Ledger and Trezor on Solana and Cardano, and from Ledger on NEAR; TRON, Sui and Aptos need a recovery-phrase account. Options and risks

Transaction review

Read it before you sign it

The Rust core decodes each transaction and shows it in a native system window, outside the app’s web view.

  • Contract calls, token approvals and high fees ask for a second confirmation.
  • On Bitcoin: unknown scripts, OP_RETURN data and fees above 100 sat/vB too.
  • Address-poisoning alerts and clipboard checks before you send.
  • Simulation on swaps and Earn calls: what you send, receive and approve, and calls that would fail.
  • Slow, Normal or Fast fees priced before you fill in the amount.
  • Speed up or cancel a pending Bitcoin, Litecoin or EVM send.

Hardware wallets

Ledger and Trezor

Keep your keys on a hardware wallet and use Kyro as the screen. Trezor Safe 7 pairs with a code.

  • Supported on Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Avalanche, Cronos, Mantle, Sonic, Flare, Kaia, Gnosis, HyperEVM, Sei, Conflux, Solana, TRON, Cardano, XRP Ledger, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Bitcoin Cash, Dash, Stellar, NEAR, Sui, TON, Cosmos Hub, Aptos, Algorand and Zcash.
  • Software accounts and hardware accounts side by side.

The small things

Many accounts

Several accounts per blockchain from the same phrase. Move funds between them in two clicks.

Tor built in

All traffic goes over Tor, with a separate circuit per blockchain. If Tor is down, Kyro does not fall back to a direct connection.

No personal data

No account and no analytics. A weekly anonymous count over Tor, which you can turn off. Every server Kyro contacts is listed on GitHub.

Auto-lock

The vault locks itself when the timer runs out, and on Windows as the computer goes to sleep. macOS and Linux lock on wake. Unlocking needs your password.

Your own servers

Point each blockchain at your own node or server. Kyro checks it is on the right network first.

Approvals overview

The vault panel shows whether any unlimited token approval is active.
